Named Nets and Signals

Net/Signal Description / Where Seen
VIN Charge input net from J1 and J3; input to U1 IN and EN.
GND Ground net used throughout schematic.
3V8 U1 output net; connected to Z1, C1, R1, and U4 VDD/EN.
3V8REGFB Feedback divider net connected to U1 ADJ, R1, and R2.
VBAT Storage/battery net connected to U4 OUT, Z3, C2, C3, U3 Vdd, and U2 VDD.
OUT_EN Enable net from U3 Vout to U2 EN.
VOUT Output net from U2 OUT to J2 and J4.
